Rise for iOS Privacy Policy

Updated 21 October 2026

Rise is a browser developed by Bartosz Bak. Questions about privacy, access to your data, or deletion can be sent to bartoszpiotrbak@gmail.com.

Browsing and local storage

Websites you visit receive normal web requests, including your IP address and any information you submit to them. Their privacy policies apply. Regular browsing stores website cookies and data, browsing history, open tabs, previews and preferences on your device. Downloads are saved in Rise’s Downloads folder in Files. “Delete File” deletes the file; clearing the downloads list leaves completed files on disk.

Rise caches generated summaries for regular pages on your device to avoid repeating requests. These caches can contain page text and generated answers and are stored as readable files in the app’s Caches directory, with a limit of 300 entries. iOS may remove caches to reclaim space.

To diagnose popup-blocking problems, Rise keeps local diagnostic logs for regular tabs: recent page and popup URL paths, allowed/blocked decisions, filter matches, gesture timing, redirects and errors. URL credentials, query values and fragments are removed, and no cookies, request headers, form bodies or page text are logged. Logs rotate over seven UTC calendar dates and are capped at 8 MB, are excluded from backup, and are never uploaded automatically. “Share popup diagnostics” in App settings exports them only when you choose to share. Private-tab diagnostics stay in memory for that private session; sharing them explicitly creates a temporary export which is removed after the share sheet closes.

Optional iCloud sync

With “Sync tabs with iCloud” enabled, regular tabs, browsing history and learned site-search information sync using your Apple account’s private CloudKit database. You can turn this off in Settings. Turning sync off does not itself erase records already in iCloud. Apple’s iCloud privacy information describes Apple’s handling of this data.

Search suggestions

As you type in the address field, Rise requests suggestions from Google. Google receives the typed search prefix and network information such as your IP address. In private tabs, Rise uses an ephemeral client without saved cookies for these requests. Submitting a search sends your query to your selected search engine. See Google’s privacy policy and the policy of any other search engine you choose.

AI is optional and requires permission

Normal browsing does not require an AI key or AI sharing permission. Page content is sent only after you explicitly allow sharing with that provider, using the permission switch in this policy panel or the first-use prompt. Entering an API key or enabling AI features alone does not grant sharing permission.

When you use these features, Rise sends the current page’s readable text, selection, title and full URL, together with your questions and relevant conversation context, to the selected provider to produce an answer. Page content may contain personal or sensitive information, including information from signed-in pages. These requests can also be made from private tabs if you allow them.

If you choose OpenRouter, OpenRouter routes requests to the model provider, currently OpenAI. If you choose OpenAI, requests go directly to OpenAI. Your API key is sent to your selected provider to authenticate requests. Each provider processes information according to its own terms, privacy and retention policies; Rise does not control their systems. Read OpenRouter’s privacy policy and OpenAI’s privacy policy before sharing sensitive content.

Keys are stored separately for each provider in this device’s Keychain and are not included in Rise’s iCloud tab sync. “Check key” contacts the selected provider to validate credentials without sending page content or generating an answer.

The “AI data sharing” switch above this policy in the app shows permission for your selected provider. Turning it on permits future AI requests to that provider. Turn it off to withdraw permission for all AI providers; turning off “AI features” in Settings also withdraws permission. Rise will ask again before a future request unless you allow sharing here. Withdrawing permission cannot recall information already sent to a provider. Delete the contents of the API key field to remove that saved key. Removing the app may leave Keychain entries, so clear keys first if you want them removed.

Private tabs

Private tabs use temporary website storage and are excluded from Rise’s saved tab sessions, history, iCloud sync, disk previews and disk summary cache. Private browsing does not hide your IP address or activity from websites or your network. It does not prevent Google suggestions or AI requests you explicitly permit. Files you download remain in Files until deleted.

Voice input

On supported versions of iOS, holding New Page records microphone audio for on-device speech transcription using Apple’s speech APIs. Permission is requested before recording. Releasing the gesture stops recording; cancelling discards the transcription. Apple may be contacted to download speech models. Transcribed text is used as your search input and can be sent to your search engine when you submit it. Voice follow-up questions you send to AI follow the AI sharing rules above.

Your choices and data removal

You can browse without AI, decline its permission prompt, withdraw permission, clear provider keys, turn off iCloud sync, close tabs, and delete downloaded files. Deleting local data does not delete copies already received by websites, AI providers or Apple. For data held by those services, use their privacy controls or contact them directly. Contact the developer at the address above for questions about Rise’s handling of your data.

Changes to data sharing will be reflected in this policy and, where needed, a new in-app permission request.
